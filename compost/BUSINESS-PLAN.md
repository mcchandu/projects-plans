---

## 1. The Market

- 500+ nurseries in Hyderabad metro — nearly all sell unbranded compost
- 200+ organic farms within 60 km of Hyderabad supplying premium retail
- Urban gardening growing 25–30% per year in Hyderabad
- GHMC maintains 1,400+ parks — all buy compost
- Branded compost sells ₹18–35/kg in Bangalore and Pune — Hyderabad is 3–5 years behind

### The Supply Gap

| Available Now | Problem |
|---|---|
| GHMC compost (₹2–3/kg) | Inconsistent quality, plastic contamination |
| Unbranded local (₹3–5/kg) | Not properly composted, no testing |
| National brands (₹12–18/kg) | Not local, limited availability |
| **Your product (₹8–16/kg)** | **Local, tested, branded — gap is empty** |

## 4. Pricing

| Product | Cost to Make | Selling Price | Margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bulk loose | ₹8–10/kg | ₹5–6/kg | 40–45% |
| 50 kg PP bag | ₹11–13/kg | ₹7.50/kg | 42% |
| 10 kg garden bag | ₹12–14/kg | ₹14/kg | 50% |
| Premium enriched (Year 2) | ₹15–17/kg | ₹20–22/kg | 30% |

**Pricing rules:**
- Never go below ₹6/kg for any bagged product
- Dealer margin: 25–30% (sell at ₹10/kg, they sell at ₹13–14/kg)
- Volume discount: 5T+ = 8% off | 10T+ = 12% off
- Peak season (Jun–Aug, Oct–Nov): hold price. Off-season (Feb–Mar): 5% pre-booking discount

| Pillar | How to Show It |
|---|---|
| **Local** | "Made in Hyderabad" badge, farm location story |
| **Tested** | QR code on bag → lab report PDF |
| **Expert-made** | Agronomist founder photo + credentials on bag |

**Free marketing that works:**

- Demo plots: give 500 kg free to 3–5 influential farmers, document yield
- WhatsApp farmer groups in your district — share tips, introduce product naturally
- ₹500 referral credit to any farmer who refers a paying customer

## 13. Risk Register

| Risk | Probability | Fix |
|---|---|---|

| Bad quality batch | Low–Medium | Strict SOPs (RUNBOOK.md); germination test every batch |
| Cash crunch in Year 1 | Medium | ₹7L working capital buffer in INVESTMENT-PLAN.md |
| Customer cancels | Medium | No single buyer >20% of revenue |
| Operator quits | Medium | RUNBOOK.md makes handover possible in 1 week |
| Monsoon site damage | Low | Drainage design, tarpaulin covers, insurance |
