## 1. The Market

### Why Now?

- Telangana farmland: ~1.44 crore hectares — even 1% adopting compost = huge demand
- 500+ nurseries in Hyderabad metro — nearly all sell imported or unbranded compost
- 200+ organic farms within 60 km of Hyderabad supplying premium retail
- Urban gardening (terrace, apartment) growing 25–30% per year
- GHMC maintains 1,400+ parks — all buy compost
- Branded compost sells ₹18–35/kg in Bangalore and Pune — Hyderabad is 3–5 years behind, **first-mover advantage is real today**

### The Supply Gap

| What's Available Now | Problem |
|---|---|
| GHMC/municipal compost (₹2–3/kg) | Inconsistent quality, plastic contamination |
| Unbranded local suppliers (₹3–5/kg) | Not composted properly, no testing |
| National brands like Biofit (₹12–18/kg) | Not locally sourced, limited Hyderabad presence |
| **Your product (₹8–16/kg)** | **Local, tested, branded — this gap is empty** |

## 2. Who Will Buy From You

### 5 Customer Types

**1. Peri-urban Organic Farmers** ⭐ Top Priority
- Farms within 80 km of Hyderabad growing vegetables/herbs/flowers
- Buy 5–20 tons per season
- Want: consistent quality, lab-tested, bulk delivery
- Reach them: direct farm visits, KVK meetings, WhatsApp farmer groups

**2. Nurseries** ⭐ Top Priority
- 500+ nurseries in Hyderabad metro
- Buy 200–500 kg/month each
- Want: 5–25 kg bags, no bad smell, reliable supply
- Reach them: walk-in visits, Instagram (nurseries are active)
- **Fastest first customer — owner decides on the spot**

**3. Urban Home Gardeners**
- Apartment residents, terrace gardeners
- Buy 10–50 kg/year
- Want: small branded bags (5–10 kg), no smell, usage instructions
- Reach them: Instagram, Facebook groups, WhatsApp

**4. Government / Institutional** (Year 2+)
- GHMC parks, corporate campuses, Hyderabad Metro landscaping
- Large orders (10–100 tons), long sales cycle (3–6 months)
- Reach them: GeM portal, direct approach to procurement officers

**5. Input Dealers (Wholesale)**
- Agri input shops in Ranga Reddy, Medak, Nalgonda districts
- Buy 2–10 tons/month, need 20–30% dealer margin
- Reach them: district agri officer lists

### Priority Order

| Segment | Start When |
|---|---|
| Nurseries + Organic Farmers | Month 1 — fastest cash |
| Input Dealers | Month 4–6 |
| Urban Online | Month 3+ |
| Government / Institutional | Month 9+ |

## 5. Pricing

### Cost vs. Selling Price

| Product | Cost to Make | Selling Price | Margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bulk (loose) | ₹9–11/kg | ₹5–6/kg | ~40–45% |
| 50 kg bag | ₹13/kg | ₹7.50/kg | ~42% |
| 10 kg garden bag | ₹14/kg | ₹14/kg | ~50% |
| Premium (Year 2) | ₹16/kg | ₹20–22/kg | ~30% |

### Pricing Rules
- Never go below ₹6/kg for any bagged product — signals low quality
- Give dealers 25–30% margin (you sell at ₹10/kg, they sell at ₹13–14/kg)
- Volume discount: 5+ tons = 8% off | 10+ tons = 12% off
- Peak season (June–Aug, Oct–Nov): hold price. Off-season (Feb–Mar): offer 5% pre-booking discount

## 6. How to Sell

### Sales Channels

```
Your Farm
   ├── Direct to organic farmers (bulk, tractor delivery)
   ├── Nurseries (weekly/fortnightly drops)
   ├── Input dealers (monthly bulk)
   ├── Online (own website + Instagram + marketplaces)
   ├── Institutional (GHMC, corporates — tenders)
   └── Farmer markets / KVK melas (brand building)
```

### Nursery Channel — Fastest Cash

**Why start here:** 500+ nurseries, owner decides on the spot, regular orders, good cash flow.

**How to open a nursery account:**
1. Walk in with 2–3 sample bags (10 kg, branded).
2. Open the bag — show texture, smell, share a printed germination test result.
3. Offer a trial: "Leave 5 bags, pay only if customers like it in 2 weeks."
4. Price to nursery: ₹10/kg (they sell at ₹13–15/kg = good margin for them).
5. Follow up on Day 14 — close the repeat order.

**Target in first 3 months:** 30 nurseries × 300 kg/month = **9 tons/month through nurseries alone**

### Online Channel

| Platform | Effort | Start When |
|---|---|---|
| Your own website | Low (₹5,000) | Month 1 |
| Instagram + WhatsApp orders | Very low | Month 1 |
| Ugaoo / UrbanKisaan | Medium | Month 3 |
| Amazon / Flipkart | High | Month 6 |
| BigBasket / Swiggy Instamart | Very high | Year 2 |

**Hyderabad tips:**
- Post in "Hyderabad Gardening" Facebook group (85,000+ members) — free
- Partner with gardening micro-influencers (5K–50K followers) for reviews
- Approach apartment RWAs in Kondapur, Gachibowli, Miyapur for bulk orders

## 7. Brand & Marketing

### Brand Positioning
> "The only compost made by an agronomist, from Telangana farms, for Telangana soil — tested, certified, delivered to your gate."

### 3 Brand Pillars
| Pillar | What It Means | How to Show It |
|---|---|---|
| **Local** | Made from Telangana farm waste | "Made in Hyderabad" badge, farm story |
| **Tested** | Every batch has a lab report | QR code on bag → lab report PDF |
| **Expert-made** | Agronomist founder | Founder photo + credentials on packaging |

### Branding Basics
- **Name ideas:** Mitti+, HaraMitti, Bhumi Organic, Rythu Compost
- **Colours:** Deep green + earthy brown + white
- **Bag design:** Product name, weight, NPK numbers, QR code, Telugu + English instructions
- **Cost:** ₹8,000–15,000 for logo + bag design

### Free Marketing — Content

Post 1 video/week on YouTube + Instagram (3–5 minutes):
- "Why your tomatoes need compost, not just DAP"
- "How we make compost — from cow dung to your farm in 10 weeks"
- "Temperature check day — how we know our compost is ready"

> **Content in Telugu is almost completely untapped.** This is a big free distribution advantage.

### Building Farmer Trust (B2B)

- Give 500 kg free to 3–5 influential farmers → document yield → share photos
- Present at KVK field days — instant credibility
- Join district WhatsApp farmer groups — share tips, introduce product naturally
- Referral reward: ₹500 credit to any farmer who refers a paying customer

## 10. Government Subsidies (Free Money — Apply Early)

| Scheme | What You Get | How to Apply |
|---|---|---|
| **PKVY** | ₹50,000/ha over 3 years for organic clusters | Through District Agriculture Officer |
| **NHM** | 50% subsidy up to ₹30,000 per vermicompost unit | State Horticulture Dept. |
| **Startup India (Agri)** | 3-year income tax holiday | startupindia.gov.in |
| **TS-iPass** | Single window business clearance | ts-ipass.telangana.gov.in |
| **Telangana Horticulture Dept.** | Organic inputs subsidy | District horticulture officer |

**How to actually get subsidies:**
1. Hire a local agri consultant for 1–2 days (₹2,000–5,000) — they know the right officer and paperwork format
2. Apply within your first 6 months — most schemes have April–June windows
3. Keep all purchase bills and photos from Day 1 — subsidies need proof
4. Join a Farmer Producer Organisation (FPO) — gets priority for benefits

## 11. Legal & Compliance

### Business Registration (Do This First)

| Step | What | Cost | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sole Proprietor or OPC registration | ₹0–5,000 | 1–3 days |
| 2 | Current bank account in business name | ₹0 | 1 day |
| 3 | Udyam / MSME registration | Free | 1 day online |
| 4 | GST registration (optional if <₹40L) | Free | 3–5 days |

### Product Compliance

| Requirement | What | How to Get |
|---|---|---|
| FCO License | Required to sell compost commercially | Apply to District Agriculture Officer + lab test |
| PGS-India Certification | Needed to say "organic" on label | Join PGS group through KVK — ₹500–2,000/year |
| Lab test per batch | NPK content for labelling | NABL-accredited lab — ₹1,500–3,000 per test |
| GST on compost | **0% (exempt)** under HSN 3101 | No action needed |

### Compliance Timeline

```
Month 1:  Udyam + bank account + sole proprietor / OPC
Month 2:  First lab test + apply for FCO license
Month 3:  PGS-India application through KVK
Month 6:  FCO license arrives (3–4 month processing)
          Start printing "FCO Compliant" on bags
```

## 12. Risks & How to Handle Them

| Risk | How Likely | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Raw material shortage | Medium | Agreements with 3+ suppliers; stockpile post-Kharif |
| Bad quality batch | Low–Medium | Strict SOPs (see RUNBOOK.md); test every batch |
| Cash crunch in Year 1 | High | Keep IT income during transition; use KCC loan |
| Big customer cancels | Medium | Never rely on 1 buyer for >20% of revenue |
| GHMC floods cheap compost | Medium | Focus on premium segment — quality gap is real |
| Operator quits | Medium | Train 2 operators; RUNBOOK.md makes handover easy |
| Monsoon damages site | Low | Proper drainage design; tarpaulin covers; insurance |
| Soil contamination from inputs | Low | Never accept unknown chemical waste; test new suppliers |
