# Master Investment Plan — ₹25 Lakhs
**Compost + Vermicompost | Farm near Hyderabad, Telangana**

---

## The Big Picture

You are investing ₹25 lakhs to build **two complementary businesses on one farm**:

| Business | Product | Price | Scale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Compost unit** | Organic compost (bagged + bulk) | ₹5–15/kg | 15 tons/month by Year 1 end |
| **Vermicompost unit** | Vermicompost + worm tea | ₹20–35/kg | 5–10 tons/month by Year 2 |

Both use the same land, same raw materials, same suppliers, and same customers.
Compost rejects feed the worm beds — zero waste between the two.
