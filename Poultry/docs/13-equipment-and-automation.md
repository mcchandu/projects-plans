# 13 Equipment and Automation

## Purpose

Equipment selection determines labor productivity, feed efficiency, hygiene quality, egg handling performance, and maintenance burden. The right level of automation should improve reliability and reduce recurring inefficiency without overcapitalizing the project.

## Equipment Categories

- Cage systems or housing equipment.
- Feeders and feed distribution mechanisms.
- Nipple drinker lines and regulators.
- Egg collection support systems.
- Brooding or pullet equipment if applicable.
- Weighing, cleaning, and utility support tools.
- Disinfection and biosecurity equipment.

## Automation Strategy

For a 10,000-layer unit, selective automation is often preferable to full automation at the first stage. Priority should be given to systems that reduce feed wastage, improve water reliability, and simplify egg collection and labor supervision.

## Recommendation

Purchase durable equipment with service support, spare availability, and field references. Cheap equipment that fails during peak production usually becomes more expensive than a slightly higher initial investment in reliable systems.
