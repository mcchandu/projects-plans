# 14 Disease Management

## Importance

Disease prevention is one of the most critical success factors in poultry farming because outbreak risk can destroy production, increase mortality, disrupt sales, and damage farm reputation. Preventive health management is always more economical than reactive treatment.

## Core Components

- Vaccination planning based on veterinary guidance.
- Controlled farm entry and sanitation protocols.
- Regular litter, manure, and waste management.
- Bird observation and early symptom reporting.
- Isolation protocol for sick or suspect birds.
- Medicine storage and administration records.
- Emergency response procedures for outbreak situations.

## Biosecurity Measures

Vehicle control, visitor restriction, footwear sanitation, rodent control, wild bird deterrence, and batch discipline should be enforced consistently. Biosecurity failure is often operational, not technical.

## Recommendation

The farm should maintain a written health calendar, veterinary contact plan, mortality log, and escalation workflow. Disease management must be embedded into daily operations from day one.
