# 08 Layer Breed Selection

## Selection Goal

Choosing the right layer breed or commercial strain is critical because productivity, feed efficiency, disease tolerance, survivability, and egg size all influence financial outcomes. Breed selection should prioritize field performance under local climate and management conditions rather than brand familiarity alone.

## Evaluation Criteria

- Hen-housed egg production potential.
- Feed consumption per bird.
- Feed conversion efficiency.
- Mortality and livability profile.
- Shell strength and egg uniformity.
- Adaptation to regional climate and housing system.
- Vendor support and chick or pullet availability.

## Preferred Approach

The farm should compare commonly available commercial layer strains from reputable suppliers and select one with reliable technical support and proven local farm results. Consistency in sourcing and vaccination history is often more important than chasing marginal differences in brochure performance.

## Recommendation

The final breed decision should be made only after consulting hatcheries, veterinarians, and nearby successful farms using similar housing and management practices. Standardization around one primary strain per cycle usually simplifies feed planning and performance monitoring.
