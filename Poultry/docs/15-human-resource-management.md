# 15 Human Resource Management

## Staffing Need

Even with good equipment, poultry production requires disciplined human supervision. Labor quality affects feed control, egg breakage, hygiene, medication accuracy, and early problem detection.

## Typical Roles

- Farm manager or supervisor.
- Shed workers and attendants.
- Egg collection and dispatch support labor.
- Security or watchman support.
- Visiting veterinarian or technical consultant.
- Maintenance and electrical support on call.

## Management Principles

Staff should work under clear SOPs, daily checklists, hygiene rules, and reporting lines. Training is especially important for egg handling, mortality reporting, medicine usage, and visitor-control discipline.

## Recommendation

A lean but accountable team is better than a larger unstructured workforce. Performance should be monitored through measurable routines, not only verbal supervision.
