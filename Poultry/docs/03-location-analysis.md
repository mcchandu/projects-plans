# 03 Location Analysis

## Regional Relevance

Chegunta and Ramayampet in Medak district offer a practical geography for a commercial poultry venture due to their proximity to agricultural belts, transport links, labor access, and regional trading movement. A farm in this belt can potentially serve nearby town demand while also accessing larger distribution routes toward Hyderabad and surrounding wholesale markets.

## Site Selection Criteria

A suitable site should satisfy the following:

- All-weather road access for feed trucks and egg dispatch vehicles.
- Sufficient setback from dense residential clusters for odor and biosecurity control.
- Reliable groundwater or supplementary water sourcing options.
- Adequate electrical connectivity and feasible transformer load access.
- Natural drainage and flood-safe topography.
- Space for future shed expansion, manure storage, and waste segregation.

## Local Advantages

The location benefits from a mixed rural-commercial environment where land-use economics are typically more favorable than peri-urban zones. Access to agricultural by-products, labor pools, and farm service providers can support both construction and daily operations.

## Risks to Evaluate

- Seasonal groundwater variation.
- Heat stress during peak summer months.
- Disease exposure from nearby poultry clusters.
- Transport delays if internal road width is poor.
- Local land-use compliance or panchayat restrictions.

## Suitability Conclusion

The Chegunta / Ramayampet region is generally suitable for a 10,000-layer farm provided the final land parcel has strong road access, assured water, drainage control, and adequate isolation from disease-prone livestock concentration. A pre-construction due diligence checklist should be completed before capex commitment.
