# 22 Five-Year Financial Projection

## Projection Objective

A five-year projection helps assess sustainability beyond the first production cycle. It should show whether the business can absorb volatility, recover capital, service debt, and improve returns over time.

## Projection Drivers

- Bird productivity and cycle management.
- Egg price assumptions.
- Feed cost escalation.
- Labor and utility inflation.
- Mortality and health performance.
- Replacement-cycle timing.
- Financing terms and interest burden.

## Projection Method

The five-year model should use a base case with annual escalation and operational learning improvements where justified. A separate downside scenario should test weaker egg prices, higher feed costs, and slower working-capital recovery.

## Interpretation

A project may appear profitable in a single-year snapshot yet remain weak across five years if replacement planning, debt servicing, and cost escalation are not modeled correctly. Long-horizon analysis is essential before taking large capital exposure.

## Recommendation

The farm should proceed only if the five-year projection remains resilient under conservative assumptions and does not depend on unusually favorable market conditions for viability.
