# 🥚 Commercial Layer Poultry Farm Business Feasibility Study

A comprehensive business feasibility study for establishing a **10,000-layer commercial poultry farm** on **2–3 acres of owned land** near **Chegunta / Ramayampet, Medak District, Telangana**.

## Overview

This repository organizes the full feasibility study into structured chapters, supporting financial models, operational references, and appendices. It is designed as a practical decision-support package for entrepreneurs, investors, banks, and agricultural institutions evaluating the commercial viability of a medium-scale layer farm.

## Project Objective
