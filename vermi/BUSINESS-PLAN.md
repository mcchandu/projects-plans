## 2. Who Will Buy

### Primary Customers — Same as Compost Business

**Nurseries** ⭐ Start here
- Already buying your compost — easy upsell on same visit
- Want 5–10 kg bags, no smell, consistent quality
- Owner decides on the spot
- Target: every nursery already buying compost from you
- 30 nurseries × 50 kg/month = 1.5T/month from Day 1 of vermi sales

**Urban Gardeners**
- Hyderabad terrace/balcony community — large and fast-growing
- Will pay ₹30–35/kg for quality product
- Best channel: Instagram, "Hyderabad Gardening" FB group (85K+ members), apartment RWAs
- Prefer 5 kg bags — lightweight, easy to carry

**Organic Vegetable Farmers**
- Already trust you from compost relationship
- Use vermicompost for high-value transplanting (tomato, capsicum, herbs)
- Buy 25 kg bags or loose per season

**Input Dealers**
- Stock vermicompost alongside your compost bags
- Higher absolute margin for them (₹5–7/kg margin on ₹25/kg vs ₹2–3/kg on ₹10/kg)
- Same delivery route as compost orders

**Institutions (Year 2+)**
- Corporate campuses, hotel gardens, GHMC nurseries
- Bulk orders, need invoice + GST

## 3. Pricing Strategy

### Why You Can Charge More

- Buyers already know vermicompost is premium — no education needed
- Lab test with NPK numbers on bag justifies price gap over regular compost
- "Worm-processed, farm-made" story works for urban buyers
- You have trust from compost relationship — upsell is a natural conversation

### Rules

- Never go below ₹18/kg for any bagged vermicompost — signals poor quality
- 5 kg urban bag: price at ₹150–175 (₹30–35/kg) — urban buyers don't compare at this level
- Dealer price: ₹18–20/kg (they sell at ₹25–28/kg = healthy margin for them)
- Bundle discount: compost 50 kg + vermicompost 10 kg together → 5% off combined order
- Seasonal: hold price in peak planting season (Jun–Aug, Oct–Nov)

## 11. Risk Register

| Risk | Probability | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Worm population crash | Low | Run 10-bed trial for 4 weeks before filling all 20 beds |
| Overheating in summer | High if no shade | Shed mandatory; mist beds daily April–May |
| Market takes longer | Medium | Own-farm use absorbs 1–2T/month; no cash lost |
| Vermi too wet at bagging | Medium | Spread in shade 1 day before bagging; target 30–40% moisture |
| Labour shortage | Medium | OPERATIONS.md SOPs allow any trained person to run beds |
| Worm tea not selling | Low | Give 10 L free to 5 nurseries in Month 9 — creates instant demand |
