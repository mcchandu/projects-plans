# Commercial Vermicompost Business Plan – ₹25 Lakh Investment

## Executive Recommendation

**Own land is available, so land purchase cost is excluded.**

With a total project budget of approximately **₹25 lakh**, the recommended project should be larger than the earlier 20-bed concept.

### Recommended Design

- **60 commercial vermicompost beds**
- **2 covered vermicompost sheds**
- Dedicated pre-composting area
- Raw material receiving and storage
- Semi-mechanized shredder
- Trommel screening
- Weighing and bagging area
- Finished goods warehouse
- Water and drainage system
- Space reserved for future expansion

## Important Financial Reality Check

A target of **₹2–3 lakh per month is possible as monthly sales revenue**, but it should not be assumed as guaranteed profit.

Bulk vermicompost generally earns much less per kg than branded and packaged products. ICAR has documented an enterprise selling loose vermicompost at ₹4,500/tonne and packaged product at ₹7,500/tonne, while premium retail products can command higher prices in some channels. citeturn0search0turn0search10

### Recommended Target

| Stage | Monthly Sales Target | Target Net Profit |
|---|---:|---:|
| First 6 months | ₹1–2 lakh | Focus on process |
| Stabilized operation | ₹2–3.5 lakh | ₹0.75–1.5 lakh |
| Premium branded business | ₹4–6 lakh | ₹2–3 lakh possible |

> **Conclusion:** If your expectation is ₹2–3 lakh NET PROFIT every month, do not depend on plain bulk vermicompost alone. Build a mixed product and sales model.

## Why 60 Beds?

The previous 20-bed design is suitable as a small commercial unit, but it may not generate enough monthly volume to support a ₹2–3 lakh monthly revenue target consistently.

With 60 beds, production can be staggered:

```text
WEEK 1 → LOAD 10 BEDS
WEEK 2 → LOAD 10 BEDS
WEEK 3 → LOAD 10 BEDS
WEEK 4 → LOAD 10 BEDS
WEEK 5 → LOAD 10 BEDS
WEEK 6 → LOAD 10 BEDS

THEN START HARVESTING THE FIRST BATCH
```

This provides a more continuous monthly production and sales cycle.

# 10. Quality and Compliance

For commercial sale, vermicompost quality must be controlled and applicable requirements under India's Fertiliser (Control) Order must be checked. Vermicompost is a notified organic fertilizer category under the FCO, and the standards include parameters such as moisture, organic carbon, nutrient content, particle size and heavy metals. citeturn0search3turn0search6turn0search37

The project should budget for:

- Batch records
- Laboratory testing
- Product labeling
- Quality control
- Current Telangana/India authorization requirements

Current rules and exemptions should be verified with the Agriculture Department before commercial sales because regulations and amendments can change. citeturn0search5turn0search35
