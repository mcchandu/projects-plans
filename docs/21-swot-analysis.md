# 21 SWOT Analysis

## Strengths

- Owned land improves long-term economics.
- Commercially relevant scale at 10,000 birds.
- Strong demand for eggs across multiple buyer segments.
- Scope for operational standardization and gradual automation.
- Secondary revenue streams support overall business value.

## Weaknesses

- High sensitivity to feed cost and market realization.
- Biological risk requires tight discipline.
- Working capital pressure can affect continuity.
- Operational success depends on management quality and records.

## Opportunities

- Expansion into institutional supply or branded local distribution.
- Addition of solar, manure monetization, or future pullet rearing.
- Better margins through efficient procurement and direct buyer relationships.
- Financing support under suitable agri-allied frameworks.

## Threats

- Disease outbreaks.
- Sharp feed inflation.
- Price crashes during oversupply periods.
- Water stress and summer heat load.
- Low-quality vendors or delayed construction execution.

## Interpretation

The SWOT profile supports investment if the project is run professionally and financed prudently. The opportunity is meaningful, but operational discipline is the difference between a profitable unit and a stressed one.
