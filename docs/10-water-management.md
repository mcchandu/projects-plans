# 10 Water Management

## Role of Water

Water quality and availability have a direct effect on bird health, feed intake, egg production, medicine administration, and hygiene. In hot climates, water planning becomes even more critical due to increased consumption and heat-stress sensitivity.

## System Requirements

The farm should provide:

- Adequate borewell or alternative source capacity.
- Raw water storage and overhead distribution.
- Filtration or treatment where required.
- Pressure-controlled drinking lines.
- Cleaning water access for sheds and service areas.
- Monitoring of microbial and mineral quality.

## Risk Factors

Poor water quality can lead to reduced intake, digestive stress, line scaling, medication inefficiency, and disease issues. Water shortage can rapidly disrupt production, especially at a 10,000-bird scale.

## Recommendation

A dedicated water plan should include source testing, storage redundancy, line-flushing SOPs, and summer contingency arrangements. Water should be managed as a critical production input, not merely a utility.
