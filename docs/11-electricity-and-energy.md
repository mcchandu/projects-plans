# 11 Electricity and Energy

## Energy Need

A layer farm requires dependable power for lighting, water pumping, feeding support systems, egg handling support equipment, office needs, and farm security. Interruption of critical electrical functions can affect bird comfort, productivity, and operational continuity.

## Required Planning

- Connected load assessment.
- Internal wiring design and safety protection.
- Backup through DG set, inverter, or hybrid solution.
- Energy-efficient lighting and motor selection.
- Provision for future automation loads.
- Solar feasibility review for daytime offset.

## Cost Perspective

Power cost may not be the single largest cost component, but outages can create disproportionately high operational risk. Therefore, resilience is often more important than only minimizing the electricity bill.

## Recommendation

The farm should maintain a clear single-line power plan, surge protection, earthing, and backup readiness for water and essential lighting loads. Solar can be evaluated as a long-term cost stabilizer once operations become steady.
