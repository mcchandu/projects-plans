# 20 Risk Analysis

## Risk Categories

The project faces multiple categories of risk that must be planned for before investment:

- Feed cost volatility.
- Egg price fluctuation.
- Disease outbreak and mortality.
- Water shortage or quality failure.
- Power disruption.
- Vendor failure or poor-quality inputs.
- Labor reliability issues.
- Regulatory or financing delays.
- Extreme weather and heat stress.

## Risk Treatment

Each major risk should be addressed with preventive controls, monitoring signals, and contingency actions. For example, feed-price risk requires procurement strategy, while disease risk requires biosecurity, vaccination, and rapid response.

## Severity View

The highest-impact risks for a layer farm typically combine biological and market shocks, especially when they occur simultaneously. A well-run farm reduces probability, but cannot eliminate volatility completely.

## Recommendation

Prepare a risk register with owner, trigger, mitigation, and response plan for each major category. Risk management should be built into governance, not handled only after a problem occurs.
