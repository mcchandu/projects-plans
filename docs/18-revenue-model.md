# 18 Revenue Model

## Primary Revenue

The principal income source is the sale of table eggs produced by the layer flock. Revenue is determined by productive bird count, laying percentage, egg quality, breakage control, and realized selling price.

## Secondary Revenue Streams

- Sale of manure or litter by-product.
- Sale of spent hens at end of productive cycle.
- Income from cracked or downgraded eggs through alternate channels where feasible.
- Possible local premium for clean grading, reliable supply, or institutional contracts.

## Revenue Planning Logic

The revenue model should not rely only on peak laying percentage or peak market price. It must incorporate normal production curves, mortality, egg breakage, off-grade output, and buyer payment cycles.

## Recommendation

Use conservative average realization values when preparing investor or bank-facing models. A diversified revenue approach improves resilience, but egg sales will remain the dominant cash engine.
