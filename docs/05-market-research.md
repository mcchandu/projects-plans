# 05 Market Research

## Demand Outlook

Eggs are a high-frequency, mass-market protein product consumed by households, hotels, bakeries, hostels, hospitals, and food-service buyers. Demand is supported by affordability, nutritional awareness, and year-round consumption patterns, though price movement can fluctuate based on feed cost, seasonality, and production cycles.

## Customer Segments

Key buyer categories include:

- Wholesale egg traders.
- Kirana and retail stores.
- Hotels, restaurants, and caterers.
- Bakeries and confectionery units.
- Institutional buyers such as hostels and hospitals.
- Local distributors serving nearby towns and villages.

## Market Entry Strategy

A new farm should avoid dependence on a single buyer. The preferred approach is to build a mix of wholesale offtake, local distributor relationships, and direct institutional supply wherever payment cycles are manageable.

## Pricing Considerations

Egg prices can move sharply based on season, demand spikes, festival patterns, transport disruption, disease news, and industry-wide supply changes. Therefore, the business model should be stress-tested against low, normal, and favorable price scenarios.

## Commercial View

The market opportunity is strong, but profitability is not guaranteed by demand alone. Farms that maintain consistent supply, clean eggs, accurate tray counts, on-time dispatch, and buyer trust usually secure better trading relationships and more stable realization.
