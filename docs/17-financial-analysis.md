# 17 Financial Analysis

## Financial Framework

The financial analysis should evaluate capital expenditure, operating cost, working capital requirement, revenue generation, debt servicing ability, and return on investment. For a poultry business, numbers should be built around realistic production assumptions rather than optimistic market peaks.

## Major Cost Heads

- Land development and civil works.
- Sheds, cages, and utility infrastructure.
- Water and electrical systems.
- Equipment and installation.
- Bird procurement or pullet acquisition.
- Feed, medicine, vaccines, and consumables.
- Labor, transport, repairs, and administration.
- Interest, contingency, and working capital.

## Financial Stress Points

Feed price inflation, delayed production ramp-up, low egg prices, elevated mortality, and longer payment cycles can all strain cash flow. The farm must therefore maintain liquidity buffers and conservative assumptions.

## Recommendation

The project should be approved only after building a detailed financial model covering base, pessimistic, and favorable scenarios. Working capital planning is as important as capex planning.
