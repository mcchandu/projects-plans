# 04 Land Utilization Plan

## Land Requirement

A **2–3 acre** parcel is adequate for a well-organized **10,000-layer commercial poultry farm** if the layout is planned with zoning discipline. The layout should balance production efficiency, ventilation, sanitation, movement control, and future growth.

## Recommended Zoning

Typical land allocation should include:

- Poultry sheds and service corridors.
- Feed storage and medicine room.
- Egg collection, grading, and dispatch area.
- Staff utility space and watchman point.
- Water storage, treatment, and pumping area.
- DG, solar, or electrical control area.
- Manure collection and compost or disposal zone.
- Internal roads, drainage, green buffer, and setback area.

## Planning Principles

Production sheds should be oriented for thermal comfort and airflow, with adequate spacing between structures to reduce disease transfer risk and improve service movement. Clean and dirty movement paths should be separated as much as possible to support biosecurity.

## Expansion Logic

If the land parcel is closer to 3 acres, the design should reserve future space for pullet rearing, a second shed block, solar installation, or expanded manure processing. If the available area is closer to 2 acres, the design must focus on efficient building footprint, safe movement lanes, and compact utility placement.

## Layout Recommendation

Prepare a scaled farm layout before civil work begins, showing shed dimensions, road widths, drainage slopes, underground lines, and fencing. Proper land planning at the beginning reduces later operational friction and expensive rework.
