# 19 Government Subsidies

## Relevance

Government support can improve project bankability by reducing financing burden, supporting capital formation, or improving access to structured agriculture lending. However, subsidy eligibility depends on scheme rules, category, project size, location, and documentation quality.

## Potential Support Areas

- Agriculture and allied-sector loan schemes.
- Poultry development support from state or central channels.
- NABARD-linked refinance or model-project guidance context.
- Subsidy or margin support under eligible entrepreneurship programs.
- Renewable energy support for solar-linked installation where applicable.

## Practical Considerations

Subsidy-led planning should never become the sole basis for project viability. Approval timelines, documentation burden, and reimbursement uncertainty should be treated carefully in cash-flow planning.

## Recommendation

The project should be structured to remain viable even without subsidy, while still pursuing eligible schemes through proper documentation. Any expected subsidy should be treated as supportive upside, not guaranteed cash.
