# 12 Poultry Shed Design

## Design Objective

Shed design must support bird comfort, airflow, hygiene, service movement, and efficient supervision. Poor shed design causes avoidable losses through heat stress, disease spread, maintenance difficulty, and reduced egg quality.

## Key Design Elements

- Proper orientation for sunlight and wind movement.
- Adequate width, height, and ventilation openings.
- Suitable cage or floor-system integration.
- Easy access for feed, inspection, and egg collection.
- Rodent and pest control considerations.
- Washability, drainage, and biosecurity-compatible entry points.

## Biosecurity Integration

Each shed should operate with controlled access, disinfectant procedures, clean footwear protocol, and separation of visitors from production areas. Structural design should help biosecurity rather than depend only on staff behavior.

## Recommendation

Use proven poultry shed dimensions and vendor-backed engineering rather than improvising during construction. A functional shed is a productivity asset and should be treated as a technical system, not simply a building.
